## [0.7.6] - 2026-06-15

Reconciles cross-harness drift in the three orchestrator `SKILL.md` files so the Kiro and Codex conductors carry the same routing guidance Claude already had. A follow-up to the v0.7.5 backward-jump fix: several SKILL.md divergences surfaced where Kiro (and in one case Codex) under-specified an instruction the other harnesses carried. None caused a guaranteed failure — the `report --stage` guidance also lives in the always-loaded stage protocol, and the engine self-corrects loud when it is omitted — but the divergence let a SKILL.md contradict the stage protocol. This brings the conductor contract back into alignment without touching the legitimate per-harness mechanism differences (question rendering, swarm fan-out, harness notes). Re-copy your `dist/<harness>/` to pick it up.

* **`report --stage <directive.stage>` is restored to the Kiro and Codex orchestrator SKILL.md** (report step, gate branches, and test-run line) — matching Claude and the shared stage protocol. Reporting the acted stage explicitly pins the transition the conductor committed, which matters in the recovery case where `Current Stage` was advanced by a direct state-tool repair.
* **Kiro's SKILL.md regains the `## Stage Graph` table** (the 32-stage human-readable mirror Claude and Codex carry) and the fuller "six directive kinds" explanation.
* **A "report once per directive; never call the state tools directly" clause is now in all three SKILL.md files**, and all three point at `docs/reference/12-state-machine.md` as the canonical state-machine reference.
* No CLI surface change; no new commands or flags. Legitimate per-harness mechanism differences (the `ask` widget vs numbered prose, the swarm fan-out mechanism, the harness-notes sections) are deliberately preserved.
