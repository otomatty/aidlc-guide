## [0.4.3] - 2026-05-22

Appends stage-protocol §13 (the Learnings Ritual) and pre-registers its companion audit event `MEMORY_EMPTY`. The ritual is the framework's human steering loop: between every stage's completion message and approval gate, the orchestrator surfaces interpretations / deviations / tradeoffs / open-questions from the per-stage memory.md and proposes promotion targets under `.claude/rules/` or `.claude/sensors/`. §13 will be rewritten to tool-as-actor once the runtime ritual handler ships in `aidlc-learnings.ts`. Audit registry grows 60 → 61 events across 16 → 17 categories; the new `### Steering Loop` category reserves capacity for `MEMORY_EMPTY` (the runtime-graph compile wires the emitter). This release also ships a placeholder `docs/reference/17-rule-system.md` skeleton (filename-derived scope, 5-layer inheritance, `enforcement: enforced` keyword, `overrides:` block schema, `pairing:` field, forward-compat policy) so the resolver and conflict detector read a frozen schema; full usage prose ships later.
