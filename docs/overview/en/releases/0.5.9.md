## [0.5.9] - 2026-06-02

Adds the commit-the-transition half of the deterministic orchestration engine — the internal `aidlc-orchestrate report` command. After the conductor acts on a directive, `report` records what happened so the next `aidlc-orchestrate next` reads fresh state. It is a thin dispatcher: it shells out to exactly one of the existing state-transition commands (approve, advance, or complete-workflow), chosen by the acted stage's gate status and then finality — it reimplements none of the transition logic. The engine stands beside the prose orchestrator and nothing in the workflow calls it yet, so your `/aidlc` runs are completely unchanged. No upgrade action required.

* New internal command `bun .claude/tools/aidlc-orchestrate.ts report --result <outcome> [--user-input <text>] [--reason <text>] [--test-run] [--project-dir <dir>]` commits the active stage's transition and prints a terminal directive. A gated stage routes through `approve` (which owns the full transition and self-advances); a non-gated stage routes through `advance`, or `complete-workflow` when it is the final in-scope stage. A rejected transition surfaces as an error directive carrying the underlying tool's message; the engine itself writes no state and holds no lock — every mutation happens inside the already-atomic state command it dispatches.
